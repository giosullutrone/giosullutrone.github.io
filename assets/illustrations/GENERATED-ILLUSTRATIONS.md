# Paper illustration provenance

These twelve original editorial illustrations were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 3 October 2026. Each was generated once and inspected. They are conceptual metaphors, not experimental figures, source documents, model outputs, or measured results. Generated people and architectural motifs are imaginary.

The original images were 1536 × 1024 PNGs. The portfolio uses optimized 1200 × 800 JPEGs. The exact prompts follow, in publication order.
