The person behind the papers

About me

Discussions with researchers or users—and things I strongly agree or disagree with in the literature—spark ideas for my papers.

I’m completing a PhD in Information and Communication Technologies at the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia, supervised by Prof. Sonia Bergamaschi. My research within the ITSERR project focuses on how LLM systems behave around safety boundaries, incomplete information, sensitive data, and external evidence.

I’m particularly interested in whether a refusal is justified, how retrieval changes a model’s decision, and whether an agent recognizes what it needs from a user before acting. I have also worked with Dr. Georgia Koutrika at DARE Lab on Text-to-SQL evaluation.
